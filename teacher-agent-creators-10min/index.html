An AI helper set up for a clear teaching or learning goal.

In our work, “pedagogical” means for teaching and learning.

Government partners

Schools & teachers

Together, we connect classroom needs, working tools and research.

07:40–08:35 | 55s 中文提示：最后升华：成果来自RPP长期共同解决问题。企业牵头、开发迭代和持续支持；大学研究；政府组织协调；学校教师真实需求与判断。强调四方互补形成单方难以独立维持的能力，企业连接课堂需求、研究与可用工具。 English speaking script: Where did these results come from? They grew through a research-practice partnership, or RPP. We work on shared problems over time. CocoRobo leads the programme, builds and improves the tools, and supports teachers. University teams bring research methods and analysis. Government partners organize local support. Schools and teachers bring real needs, trials and teaching judgment. Together, we can connect these strengths in ways each partner would struggle to sustain alone. The company turns classroom needs and research ideas into tools people can use. Feedback then guides the next design.
