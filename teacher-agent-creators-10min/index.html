2 · RESEARCH / FINDING 1

Building agents makes teaching ideas visible

Teachers explain their choices in a design. They can then test it and change it.

Make choices clear

Define the agent’s role, steps and limits.

Learn through testing

See what it does. Discuss it. Improve the design.

Study what teachers build, as well as what they say. This gives us a view of AI-TPACK in practice.

05:40–06:20 · 40s 中文提示：发现一：做智能体，让教学判断变成可看、可试、可改的设计。 English speaking script: The first finding is about learning by making. When teachers build an agent, they must explain their teaching choices. What should it do? What should it leave to the student? They can then test the agent and change the design. This makes their ideas easier to see and discuss. We can study what they build, as well as what they say. 2 · RESEARCH / DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

Good AI design protects students’ thinking

AI gives answers too soon

Ask students to try first.

AI replaces students’ own words

Ask for peer feedback first.

AI stops a useful discussion

Pause AI and let the discussion continue.

Teachers need the power to delay, limit or pause AI.

07:45–08:25 · 40s 中文提示：设计启示：先尝试、先同伴、必要时暂停AI。保护学生要做的思考。 English speaking script: Here are three design choices that follow from this idea. If AI gives answers too soon, ask students to try first. If AI replaces their own words, ask for peer feedback first. If AI stops a useful discussion, pause it. These are ways to protect student thinking. Teachers need the power to make these choices. Sometimes good AI use means using less AI. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR PROFESSIONAL LEARNING

Three priorities for education systems

  1. Let teachers build and test

    Make agents. Try them. Improve them.

  2. Look at designs and changes

    Use the work itself, as well as surveys.

  3. Give support and room to decide

    Provide time and peer support. Let teachers limit AI.

08:25–08:55 · 30s 中文提示：三个建议：让教师动手做；看设计和修改；给时间、同伴和决策权。 English speaking script: For education systems, I suggest three priorities. Let teachers build and test agents. Look at their designs and how they change them. Give teachers time, peer support and the right to limit AI. These are practical ways to support teacher agency: the ability to make choices and act on them.
