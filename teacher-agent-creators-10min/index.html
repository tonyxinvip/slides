A SHARED STARTING POINT

What is a pedagogical AI agent?

An AI helper set up for a clear teaching or learning goal.

In our work, “pedagogical” means for teaching and learning.

Teachers shape it

They set its role, materials, steps and limits.

It supports learning

It asks questions, guides practice and gives feedback.

Why use one?

Turn a teaching plan into support that can be tested, reused and revised.

English speaking practice · Pingshan

The teacher sets the purpose. The agent provides the agreed support.

00:20–01:15 | 55s 中文提示：概念：有明确教学目标的AI助手。教师设角色、材料、步骤、限制。用途：提问、练习、反馈。必要性：把教学计划变成可测试、复用、修改的支持。 English speaking script: First, what do I mean by a pedagogical AI agent? It is an AI helper with a clear teaching or learning goal. A teacher sets its role, materials, steps and limits. For example, an English practice agent can ask a question, listen and give feedback. Why use one? It turns a teaching plan into support that students can use. Teachers can test that support, use it again and change it. The purpose comes from the teacher. [Sources] Working definition used in this talk, based on the presenter’s teacher-designed agent programme. This is an operational definition, not a claim that all pedagogical agents have the same architecture or are fully autonomous. Photo and English-class example: Pingshan Education Bureau, AI-Empowered Education source deck, slide 1 (district-s1-img2.jpeg). Reuse and revision are design affordances, not claims of proven learning gains.

2 · RESEARCH / FINDING 1

Building agents makes teaching choices visible

A teaching idea becomes a design that can be tested and changed.

Make choices clear

What should the agent do? What should the student do?

Learn through testing

Test it. Discuss what happens. Improve it.

Teacher-made application · city contest example

Look at what teachers build and revise, as well as what they report.

05:55–06:30 | 35s 中文提示：发现一：教学判断变成设计，就能运行、测试、讨论和修改。图为作品示例；研究结论仍来自前页说明的研究。 English speaking script: The first finding is about learning by making. Teachers must explain their choices when they build an agent. What should it do? What should it leave to the student? They can test the design and change it. Their teaching ideas become easier to see and discuss. So we should study what teachers build and revise, as well as what they say. Teachers learn to decide when AI should help—and when it should step back.

01

07:10–08:00 | 50s 中文提示：发现汇总为边界学习：任务、模型能力、责任、具体设计。例子：AI答太早，就先让学生尝试。框架仍在发展，目的保护学生思考。 English speaking script: Together, these findings lead us to a working idea called Boundary Learning. WHAT OTHERS CAN TAKE AWAY

Three ways to support teacher agency

Teachers shape AI’s role in learning.

1

Create room to build

Offer access, real teaching tasks and chances to test agents.

2

Learn from designs and revisions

Use teacher-made work and peer review to guide learning.

3

Support teachers over time

Provide time and help. Let teachers limit or pause AI.

Support teachers as designers and decision-makers.

08:00–08:35 | 35s 中文提示：收束实践与研究：能动手做、从作品与修改中学习、长期有支持且能决定AI边界。教师是设计者和决策者。 English speaking script: So what can others take away? First, give teachers access and real chances to build. Second, use their designs, tests and revisions as part of professional learning. Third, provide support over time, and let teachers limit or pause AI. These lessons connect our practice and research. They help teachers become both designers and decision-makers.
