02. What is a pedagogical AI agent?
00:20–01:15 | 55s
中文提示：概念：有明确教学目标的AI助手。教师设角色、材料、步骤、限制。用途：提问、练习、反馈。必要性：把教学计划变成可测试、复用、修改的支持。

English speaking script:
First, what do I mean by a pedagogical AI agent? It is an AI helper with a clear teaching or learning goal. A teacher sets its role, materials, steps and limits. For example, an English practice agent can ask a question, listen and give feedback. Why use one? It turns a teaching plan into support that students can use. Teachers can test that support, use it again and change it. The purpose comes from the teacher.


03. One goal. Two views.
01:15–01:50 | 35s
中文提示：承接概念：教师能动性=有能力、有空间作教学选择。先实践四案例，再研究教师学习。整体工作覆盖四区和合作学校、千余教师。

English speaking script:
This brings us to teacher agency. In simple words, teachers need the ability and room to make teaching choices. We will look at this in two ways. First, practice: four cases at different levels. Then, research: what teachers learn and what keeps them creating. Our wider teacher development work reached over one thousand teachers across four districts and partner schools.


04. Shenzhen: access and chances to create
01:50–02:30 | 40s
中文提示：深圳：10个平台共同入口＋三届开发大赛、两千余教师。启示：工具入口与创作机会一起提供。

English speaking script:
First, the city level. Shenzhen built a shared platform called Shenjiao AI. It brings together ten approved learning platforms. The city also held three contests for teacher-made applications. More than two thousand teachers took part. This gives teachers both access to tools and a chance to create. The lesson for other cities is to connect access with teacher creation.


05. Pingshan: help teachers lead AI use
02:30–03:10 | 40s
中文提示：从市到区：有入口，还需要教师学习。三批教师，从提示词到智能体；教师交流设计并修改。课堂AI反馈、教师定标准。

English speaking script:
Access is the first step. Teachers also need ways to learn. In Pingshan, three groups moved from writing prompts to building teaching agents. They shared their designs and improved them. In the English class shown earlier, AI gives feedback. Teachers set the standards. The lesson is to develop teachers as designers who decide how AI should help.


06. Liyuan: teachers shape the standards
03:10–03:55 | 45s
中文提示：从区到集团：不只培训技能，还共同定标准。专家初拟、教师评分、团队定稿、开发工具、课堂试用。

English speaking script:
Skills matter. Shared standards matter too. At Liyuan Education Group, experts first drafted classroom criteria. Teachers rated them. The team reviewed the results and agreed on the final set. These criteria became an AI tool for classroom observation. The team tried it in lessons and revised it with teacher feedback. The lesson is to involve teachers in the standards that guide AI.


07. Bao’an: give each agent a clear job
03:55–04:40 | 45s
中文提示：从集团到学科：标准落实到真实任务。罗老师TASK：命题、测练、标准匹配、数据反馈。教学方法在前，AI分工在后。

English speaking script:
Standards become useful when they guide a real task. In Bao’an, Ms Luo designed an English speaking method called TASK. Her workflow has four AI agents. T helps design speaking tasks. A supports practice and assessment. S matches activities to the standards. K gives feedback from learning data. Each has a clear job. Start with the teaching method. Then decide where AI can help.


08. AI supports the task. Students still think.
04:40–05:15 | 35s
中文提示：实践收束：所有支持最终落到课堂。生物探究中，AI辅助，教师设限，学生解释和判断。过渡：教师在这些实践中学到了什么？

English speaking script:
Across these cases, the aim is support that fits the classroom. In this biology example, AI helped students improve an inquiry design. It did not give the final answer. Students still had to explain their ideas and make a judgment. The teacher set the limits. These cases show what we can put in place. But what do teachers learn through this work?


09. What did we study about teacher learning?
05:15–05:55 | 40s
中文提示：明确进入研究：四个案例是实践展示；研究证据来自坪山三批教师。作品、日志、教师陈述相互补充。不把参与规模当作学习成效。

English speaking script:
I will now turn to the research. The four cases are examples of practice. Our research evidence comes from three teacher groups in Pingshan. We studied their agents, design changes and platform use. We also used surveys and interviews. This lets us look at both what teachers say and what they do. It does not prove that AI improved student learning.


10. Building agents makes teaching choices visible
05:55–06:30 | 35s
中文提示：发现一：教学判断变成设计，就能运行、测试、讨论和修改。图为作品示例；研究结论仍来自前页说明的研究。

English speaking script:
The first finding is about learning by making. Teachers must explain their choices when they build an agent. What should it do? What should it leave to the student? They can test the design and change it. Their teaching ideas become easier to see and discuss. So we should study what teachers build and revise, as well as what they say.


11. Teachers need support to keep creating
06:30–07:10 | 40s
中文提示：发现二：参加培训不等于持续创设。选择、能力、归属感、学校支持。核心：要持续支持教师，而非只完成一次培训。

English speaking script:
The second finding is about continued creation. Some teachers kept building and revising. Others mainly explored. Some took little part. Training alone did not make everyone an active creator. The studies point to choice, skills, belonging and school support. Teachers need time and people they can ask for help. Building an agent is one step. Keeping teachers able to create is a longer task.


12. Boundary Learning: deciding where AI belongs
07:10–08:00 | 50s
中文提示：发现汇总为边界学习：任务、模型能力、责任、具体设计。例子：AI答太早，就先让学生尝试。框架仍在发展，目的保护学生思考。

English speaking script:
Together, these findings lead us to a working idea called Boundary Learning. It means learning where AI belongs. We ask four questions. Is the task right for AI? Can it do the task well? Who should make the key decisions? How should we set its roles and limits? For example, if AI answers too soon, ask students to try first. The framework is still developing. Its purpose is to help teachers protect student thinking.


13. Three ways to support teacher agency
08:00–08:35 | 35s
中文提示：收束实践与研究：能动手做、从作品与修改中学习、长期有支持且能决定AI边界。教师是设计者和决策者。

English speaking script:
So what can others take away? First, give teachers access and real chances to build. Second, use their designs, tests and revisions as part of professional learning. Third, provide support over time, and let teachers limit or pause AI. These lessons connect our practice and research. They help teachers become both designers and decision-makers.
