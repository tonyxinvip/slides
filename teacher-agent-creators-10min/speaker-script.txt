02. What is a pedagogical AI agent?
00:20–01:05 | 45s
中文提示：概念：有明确教学目标的AI助手。教师设角色、材料、步骤、限制。用途：提问、练习、反馈。必要性：把教学计划变成可测试、复用、修改的支持。

English speaking script:
First, what is a pedagogical AI agent? It is an AI helper with a clear teaching or learning goal. A teacher sets its role, materials, steps and limits. For example, it can guide English practice and give feedback. Why use one? It turns a teaching plan into support we can test, use again and improve. The purpose comes from the teacher.

English speaking script:
Our goal is teacher agency: the ability and room to make teaching choices. First, I will share practice, from policy to classroom design. Then, I will share research on teacher learning. Our wider programme reached over one thousand teachers.

06. Pingshan: help teachers lead AI use

07. Liyuan: teachers shape the standards
03:20–03:55 | 35s

English speaking script:
Skills matter. Shared standards matter too. At Liyuan Education Group, experts drafted classroom criteria. Teachers rated them. The team agreed on the final set. These criteria became an AI classroom-observation tool. The team tried it in lessons and revised it with teacher feedback. Teachers helped shape the standards that guide AI.

09. Why does practice need research?
04:30–05:15 | 45s
中文提示：关键转折：能做出案例还不够。需要理解教师判断如何发展、持续创作需要哪些条件、换场景怎样调整。研究的用途是改进实践并形成可借鉴经验。

English speaking script:
These cases show what we can build. But a working example cannot tell us everything. In this biology lesson, teachers still need to decide how much help AI should give. We need research to understand how teachers learn that judgment. What helps them keep creating? What must change in another school? Evidence helps us improve the tools and training, and share lessons others can use.


10. A year of practice, evidence and revision
05:15–06:00 | 45s
中文提示：12个月、坪山三群体：2025年4月→9月→2026年4月。多源证据。4篇会议论文接受：AERA 2篇、ISLS 2篇。期刊边界学习仍在推进。不是把四个实践案例当四个研究群体。

English speaking script:
Our research follows three teacher groups in Pingshan over twelve months. It began with prompts and first agents. It grew into subject groups and workflows. We then deepened the study of teacher learning. We used designs, revisions, platform use, surveys and interviews. Four conference papers were accepted: two at AERA and two at ISLS. Our Boundary Learning journal paper is still in progress. Each round helps improve the next.


11. Building agents makes teaching choices visible
06:00–06:30 | 30s
中文提示：发现一：教学判断变成设计，就能运行、测试、讨论和修改。图为作品示例；研究结论仍来自前页说明的研究。

English speaking script:
The first finding is about learning by making. Building an agent makes teaching choices visible. What should AI do? What should students do? Teachers can test those choices and change the design. We can then study how their ideas develop. This gives us more than a survey of how confident they feel.


12. Teachers need support to keep creating
06:30–07:00 | 30s
中文提示：发现二：参加培训不等于持续创设。选择、能力、归属感、学校支持。核心：要持续支持教师，而非只完成一次培训。

English speaking script:
The second finding is about continued creation. Some teachers kept building. Others explored, or took little part. Training alone did not make everyone an active creator. Teachers need choice, skills, trusted peers and school support. These findings help us redesign professional learning, with time and help beyond the workshop.


13. Boundary Learning: deciding where AI belongs
07:00–07:40 | 40s
中文提示：发现汇总为边界学习：任务、模型能力、责任、具体设计。例子：AI答太早，就先让学生尝试。框架仍在发展，目的保护学生思考。

English speaking script:
Together, these studies lead to a working idea called Boundary Learning. Teachers learn where AI belongs. Is the task right for AI? Can it do it well? Who should decide? What roles and limits should we set? If AI answers too soon, for example, ask students to try first. This framework is still developing. It helps keep student thinking at the centre.


14. Four partners make the work possible
07:40–08:35 | 55s
中文提示：最后升华：成果来自RPP长期共同解决问题。企业牵头、开发迭代和持续支持；大学研究；政府组织协调；学校教师真实需求与判断。强调四方互补形成单方难以独立维持的能力，企业连接课堂需求、研究与可用工具。

English speaking script:
Where did these results come from? They grew through a research-practice partnership, or RPP. We work on shared problems over time. CocoRobo leads the programme, builds and improves the tools, and supports teachers. University teams bring research methods and analysis. Government partners organize local support. Schools and teachers bring real needs, trials and teaching judgment. Together, we can connect these strengths in ways each partner would struggle to sustain alone. The company turns classroom needs and research ideas into tools people can use. Feedback then guides the next design.


15. Make the partnership useful in practice
08:35–09:10 | 35s
中文提示：将模式转成行动：共同定义真实教学问题；创作—研究证据—改进循环；支持会议之外的技术支持、协调和教师时间。论文、工具和更好的教学判断都是价值。

English speaking script:
So what can others take away from this model? Start with a shared teaching problem. Let teachers help define it. Connect creation, evidence and redesign. Support the work between meetings, including technical help and teacher time. Papers matter. So do better tools and teaching choices. This is how partnership can turn local practice into useful knowledge.
