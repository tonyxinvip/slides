07. Liyuan: teachers shape the standards
集团AI成果展示(1).zip → 荔园教育集团AI教育成果展示-英文版.pptx, slide 3; Chinese version, slide 3 embedded process graphic. Teacher support described in slide 2 and PPT稿件中英文版.docx.

Evidence and interpretation:
The five-stage process describes expert drafting of a four-level indicator system, scoring by about 50 teachers, finalization by a nine-person research group, AI model and tool development, and classroom testing with revision using teacher feedback. Do not present the design and trial process as established measurement validity. The support system also includes self-study micro-courses, group training, visiting coaching and technical Q&A. International recognition and pilot-school claims are not needed for this takeaway.
The “Lesson to share” is our interpretation of the documented approach, not a measured causal finding.

14. Four partners make the work possible
Programme partnership and roles: https://cocorobo.hk/unesco2026/teacher/ , sections An RPP-informed Model and Collaboration note; presenter confirmation in this conversation. Partners include university research teams from CUHK, HKU and EdUHK, district education partners, schools and teachers; do not imply each university coauthored every paper or the provincial government is a formal partner in the specific research programme. CocoRobo’s documented role includes programme leadership, technical implementation, platform iteration, teacher training and PD redesign.
RPP conceptual grounding: William T. Grant Foundation, About RPPs, https://rpp.wtgrantfoundation.org/about/ , drawing on Coburn, Penuel and Geil (2013): sustained collaboration, jointly defined problems, mutual benefit, research production and use. This deck interprets the documented programme through an RPP lens; four stakeholder types alone do not define an RPP.
Complementarity and the value of enterprise participation are the presenter’s analysis of the mode of work, not a controlled comparison, a historical first or measured superiority over single-sector projects. Research independence, consent and teachers’ pedagogical judgment remain important to the model.
