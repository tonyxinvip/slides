2 · RESEARCH / FINDING 1

Creating agents makes pedagogy visible

Teachers turn teaching judgments into runnable designs that can be tested and revised.

Make the judgment explicit

Specify the agent’s role, sequence and limits.

Examine what the agent does

Use tests and design revisions to inspect the pedagogy.

AI-TPACK can be examined in design traces, alongside teachers’ self-reports.

04:15–05:00 · 45s 中文提示：发现一：创设让隐性教学判断显性化；看作品和修改，而不只问自信。 English speaking script: The first finding concerns learning through creation. Building an agent asks teachers to make their teaching judgments explicit: what should it do, in what sequence, and with what limits? Testing and revision then make those choices available for reflection. We can examine the integration of AI, pedagogy and subject knowledge in the design itself. This gives us evidence of competence beyond asking teachers how confident they feel about using AI. Active creators

After training, teachers showed different patterns: active creation, browsing, or low engagement. The studies also point to autonomy, competence, relatedness and school support. 2 · RESEARCH / AN EMERGING FRAMEWORK

Boundary Learning: deciding how AI participates

Task

Where is AI useful, and where is human effort essential?

Model

Does a problem come from the prompt, workflow or model?

Responsibility

Which decisions belong to teachers, students or peers?

Configuration

Turn these judgments into roles, limits and timing.

A working synthesis of teacher judgment, still under development.

05:40–06:25 · 45s 中文提示：现在才提出边界学习：任务、模型、责任、配置。这是发展中的框架。 English speaking script: These studies lead us to an emerging framework called Boundary Learning. It brings together four judgments. First, where is AI useful for the task? Second, what can the model actually do, and where does it fail? Third, which responsibilities belong to teachers, students and peers? Fourth, how do we turn those judgments into roles, constraints and timing? This is a working synthesis, still under development, rather than a fully validated theory. [Sources] Adapted from original deck, slide(s) 3, 14: https://tonyxinvip.github.io/slides/teacher-agent-creators/ Programme evidence and source links: https://cocorobo.hk/unesco2026/teacher/

2 · RESEARCH / DESIGN IMPLICATIONS

Good AI design protects students’ thinking

AI gives the answer too early

Require a student attempt before feedback.

AI replaces the student’s voice

Use peer critique before AI-assisted revision.

AI closes down discussion

Constrain or pause AI to restore dialogue.

Teachers need the authority to delay, constrain or remove AI participation.

06:25–07:05 · 40s 中文提示：把框架讲具体：先尝试、先同伴、必要时关闭AI。这里是设计启示，不是新增实验结果。 English speaking script: The framework becomes concrete in design choices. If AI gives answers too early, require a student attempt first. If it replaces student voice, bring in peer critique before AI revision. If it ends a discussion, pause its participation. These are design implications, not additional experimental findings. The aim is to preserve the effort and dialogue that learning requires, and give teachers the authority to make those choices. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR PROFESSIONAL LEARNING

Three priorities for education systems

  1. Organize learning around creation

    Teachers build, test and revise pedagogical agents.

  2. Assess designs and revisions

    Use artifacts and traces alongside self-report.

  3. Sustain support and teacher authority

    Provide time and peer support; allow teachers to limit AI.

07:05–07:35 · 30s 中文提示：收束：做作品、看证据、给支持；让教师有权决定AI的参与。 English speaking script: For education systems, I would emphasize three priorities. Organize professional learning around creation. Assess the designs and revisions teachers produce. And sustain the time, peer support and decision-making authority they need. Together, these move professional development toward teachers actively shaping how AI participates in education.
